Your information

Privacy policy

How we handle your personal information when you visit our website, contact us or arrange a performance.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

This website promotes the Melvin Beddow Big Band. The Melvin Beddow Big Band is responsible for deciding how your personal information is used and is the data controller.

For questions about your information, email enquiries@mbbb.co.uk .

Information we collect

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your proposed event date, venue and type of event.
  • Your messages and our correspondence with you.
  • Booking and payment records where you arrange a performance.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or details about other people unless these are needed for your enquiry.

Our hosting and website service providers may also process technical information, such as your IP address, browser details and the time of a request, when delivering and protecting the website.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and discuss availability.
  • Provide quotations and arrange performances.
  • Communicate with you about your booking.
  • Maintain necessary business and financial records.
  • Operate, maintain and protect our website.

Our reasons for using your information

For enquiries about a booking you intend to make yourself, and for managing that booking, we use information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to fulfil that contract.

For general correspondence, enquiries made on behalf of an organisation and website security, we rely on our legitimate interests in running the band, responding to people who contact us and keeping our services secure.

Where we must retain information to meet a legal requirement, such as applicable accounting obligations, we rely on that legal obligation.

Testimonials

We publish testimonials with the contributor's permission. These may include their name, comments, event type and event date. You can withdraw that permission by contacting us, and we will remove the testimonial from our website.

Published testimonials are publicly accessible and may be indexed by search engines or copied by others.

Who receives your information

Relevant band representatives may access information where necessary to respond to you or arrange a performance. Service providers supporting our email, website hosting and technical maintenance may also process information for those purposes.

Our website is hosted by Mythic Beasts and maintained by Dunmow Computer Services.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to obtain professional advice about a booking or dispute.

Website services and external links

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on relevant pages, JavaScript from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to these services when loading those resources, which exposes technical information such as your IP address to their providers.

Links to ticket sellers, venues, YouTube and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Information you provide to those services is handled under their policies.

This website does not uses cookies, browser storage or analytics. It does link to external services that may use cookies, such as YouTube and ticket sellers. Please check their privacy policies for details.

International processing

Third-party services may process information outside the UK, including in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United States. We will only transfer information to a country outside the UK if it is protected by an appropriate legal safeguard, such as the EU-U.S. Privacy Shield or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep information

We will keep your information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage any booking and meet our legal obligations.

Technical logs and other information collected by our hosting and website service providers are retained for a limited period to support the operation and security of the website.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and our reason for using your information, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of your personal information.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To make a request, email enquiries@mbbb.co.uk . We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

Questions and complaints

Please contact us if you have concerns about how we handle your information. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Visit the ICO's complaints page for information about making a complaint.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. The date above shows when it was last updated.
